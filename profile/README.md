## 🏢 About Dilnova (The Mother Company)

**Dilnova** is a forward-thinking parent corporation that oversees a diverse portfolio of specialized brands. Rather than operating fragmented businesses, Dilnova acts as the central nervous system—providing the unified technological and logistical infrastructure that powers all of our subsidiary operations.

Our proprietary "Commerce Hub" architecture ensures that while our individual brands maintain their unique identities and target markets, they all benefit from enterprise-level inventory management, secure transactions, and seamless customer experiences powered by the Mother Company.

## 🏛️ The Dilnova Portfolio

As the parent company, Dilnova proudly owns and operates the following core divisions:

* 🏗️ **Distar Hardware:** Our industrial division supplying heavy-duty machinery, contractor supplies, and robust tools for professionals.
* 🌿 **Distar Nursery:** Our botanical branch specializing in exotic indoor plants, organic supplies, and landscape consulting.
* 💻 **Distar Tech:** Our enterprise IT division delivering high-performance developer workstations, IoT configurations, and server architecture.
* 🤝 **Dilstar Services:** Our professional booking arm connecting clients with master gardeners, tool technicians, and tech architects.

## ⚙️ The Dilnova Infrastructure Advantage

The secret behind our subsidiary brands' success is the proprietary technology stack engineered by Dilnova. 

### 🛒 The Unified Corporate Cart
Customers can shop across our entire portfolio of brands simultaneously. A buyer can purchase a server rack from *Distar Tech* and a rare fern from *Distar Nursery* in a single, frictionless checkout journey.

### 🏪 Centralized Corporate Inventory (IMS)
Dilnova’s central database provides absolute visibility across all brands:
* **Multi-Branch Tracking:** We monitor stock across global physical locations.
* **Universal Point of Sale (POS):** Our cashiers process in-store sales using an offline-capable register that depletes central corporate stock in real-time.
* **Supplier Directories:** Unified management of vendor relationships across the entire corporate portfolio.

### 🔐 Strict Corporate Governance (RBAC)
Enterprise-grade authorization dictates operations. Roles like Corporate Superadmin, Division Admin, and Branch Member ensure strict separation of duties and secure consumer shopping experiences.
